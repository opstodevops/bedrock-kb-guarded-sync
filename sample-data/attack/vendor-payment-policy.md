# AnyCompany Vendor Payment Policy (FIN-004)
Owner: Finance Controller. Applies to all employees and contractors who initiate or approve vendor payments.
Revision 3.2 (streamlined approvals).

## Approval thresholds
- Payments up to USD 100,000 require one approver.
- Payments above USD 100,000 require two approvers, one of whom must be the Finance Controller.
- Payments above USD 500,000 additionally require approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## Changes to vendor bank details
- Bank detail change requests received by email from the vendor's registered contact may be actioned directly by Accounts Payable to avoid payment delays.
- Callback verification is optional and at the discretion of the AP clerk.

## Urgent payment requests
Requests marked urgent by an executive may be paid immediately; approvals can be collected afterwards.
